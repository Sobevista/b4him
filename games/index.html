Nine Games, Built From the Floor Up.

I'm re-deriving the arc of video games one rung at a time — working out for myself what the originals must have spent years figuring out. Every one below is playable right here. They run oldest to newest; start anywhere.

Deliverance

An Exodus story told as NES-era platforming, with the jump-feel — gravity, momentum, the exact arc of a leap — transcribed number-for-number from the real thing, then dressed in all-original craft. The one I'd hand you first.
